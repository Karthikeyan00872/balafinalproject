Procedure

Step-by-step online application flow

1

Access Portal & Register CAN

Visit tnesevai.tn.gov.in or tnedistrict.tn.gov.in, use Citizen Login, then search or register your CAN.

2

Fill Application

Select REV-201, add personal, family, income, destitute, and Aadhaar-linked bank details.

3

Upload Documents

Upload Aadhaar, smart ration card, age proof, income certificate, affidavit, bank passbook, and photo.

4

Submit & Track

Submit, save the acknowledgement reference number, and wait for VAO, RI, and Tahsildar verification.

Documents

Required upload checklist

  • Aadhaar Card & Smart Ration Card
  • Age Proof: Voter ID, Birth Certificate, or SSLC card
  • Income Certificate from Tahsildar / Revenue Department
  • Self-Declaration / Destitute Affidavit
  • Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook Copy
  • Passport-size Photograph
