Unorganised Workers Welfare

Digital registration, verification, claims, and smart-card platform

A final-year engineering MVP modeled after the Tamil Nadu Unorganised Workers Welfare Board portal, supporting OTP login, document uploads, officer verification, scheme claims, and QR smart cards.

Explore Workflow

Registration Flow

Worker onboarding from OTP to smart card

1

Mobile OTP Login

Workers verify their 10-digit mobile number before creating or resuming an application.

2

Board & Occupation

Select a welfare board such as Construction, Tailoring, or Auto/Taxi Driver and the matching occupation.

3

Profile & Documents

Capture personal, address, bank, nominee, and family details with Aadhaar, ration card, passbook, photo, and work certificate uploads.

4

Verification & Card

Applications move through Pending Verification, Field Verification, Approved or Rejected, and Smart Card Issued statuses.

Claims

Scheme claim services

  • Education allowance claims with bonafide certificates
  • Marriage grant claims with invitation and identity documents
  • Maternity benefit claims with medical certificates
  • Pension and accident relief claims with supporting records
  • Tracking IDs, officer remarks, and real-time status updates
